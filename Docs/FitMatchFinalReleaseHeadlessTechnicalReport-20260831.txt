## Confirmed product corrections retained

### Result recompare immutability

RecommendationResultView begins a new comparison from a detached,
server-authorized target/reference context. It does not write to the displayed
completed Product/reference while a recompare is pending. Global and valid
server-backed USER_EXPLICIT paths create a new Result/History after completion;
new comparison failure leaves the old Result/History untouched.

### Exact historical V4 projection

VNextHistoryCacheHydrator and RecommendationHistoryStore use a unique immutable
projection for each completed comparison. Historical audience, tuple,
USER_EXPLICIT provenance/revision, target identity, and reference identity do
not flatten to current Global state or share mutable Product meaning.

### Atomic effective tuple

VNextRuntimeClassificationTuple consumes a present server effective tuple as
issued. A nullable/not-applicable personal axis is never coalesced from a
conflicting Global field.

### Entry and Share safety

Home/Compare invalid, malformed, unsupported, and COS links return the current
meaningful blocked state without parser/server work. COS is not advertised.
Shared/FitMatchSharedURLHandoff.swift is the single app/extension handoff
contract: atomic Codable payload, fixed 15-minute TTL, malformed fail-closed,
and generation-aware acknowledgement so A cannot clear newer B.

### History visibility and Home identity

History hide is a durable user-owned visibility layer; it never mutates
completed comparison evidence. Home recent history preserves different
completed comparison identities even when product URL/name match. History
search/filter/sort/favorite is approved existing flow authority, not a newly
invented feature: Docs/flows/README.md, Docs/flows/00_UserActionIndex.md
ACT-HISTORY-003, and Docs/flows/14_HistoryFlow.md.

### Account cache isolation

Both sync coordinators bind pending work to the active owner. An old A response
is discarded after B is prepared; a newer same-owner request gets the required
follow-up pass. ContentView gates presentation/sync until local ownership
preparation completes.
