## Confirmed remediation

1. **Result recompare immutability:** starting 다른 옷과 비교 creates a
   detached current server-authorized target and reference context. It never
   mutates the displayed completed Result or its historical reference.
2. **V4 historical projection:** hydration and local persistence use a
   per-comparison immutable target/reference projection. Audience, tuple,
   USER_EXPLICIT provenance, revision, and reference identity are retained.
3. **Home recent History identity:** completed comparison identity, rather than
   URL/name, controls the recent History presentation.
4. **Home URL explanation:** empty, malformed, unsupported, and COS links fail
   closed with an actionable current-provider explanation and no parser call.
5. **Share handoff integrity:** app and extension use one Codable App Group
   payload with 15-minute TTL and generation-aware acknowledgement.
6. **Account switch cache isolation:** stale outgoing-account Closet/History
   remote responses cannot hydrate after a newer account is prepared.
