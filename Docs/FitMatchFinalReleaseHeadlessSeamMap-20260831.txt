## Physical-only boundary retained

No seam was added merely to impersonate Apple/system UI. CR-019, EN-002,
EN-007, and EN-012 retain only physical presentation/gesture/system-Share
validation. Their production data/state/routing actions are headless-callable
and already tested.

## Integrity statement

The seams are small action/coordinator extractions, not a second app state
machine. Views and tests share them; scorer invocation remains behind the
production server-authorized begin sequence.
